[2026-09-11] Random video start position
---------------------------------------------------
- Modified: static/js/preloader.js
  - Added _setupRandomStart(vid, displayInterval) helper function
    that listens for 'loadedmetadata' and sets currentTime to a
    random position, leaving at least displayInterval seconds
    before the end of the video
  - setCurrent(url, mediaType, volumeSettings, displayInterval):
    for videos, waits for metadata, seeks to random position,
    then calls play() (instead of playing immediately from 0:00)
  - preloadNext(url, mediaType, volumeSettings, displayInterval):
    pre-seeks preloaded video to random position so it's ready
    when swapped in
  - swap(nextUrl, nextType, volumeSettings, displayInterval):
    passes displayInterval through to internal preloadNext call
- Modified: static/js/app.js
  - showCurrent(): loads settings before calling setCurrent, passes
    settings.interval
  - scheduleNext(): loads settings before calling preloadNext, passes
    settings.interval
  - transition(): loads settings before calling swap, passes
    settings.interval
- Reason: Videos always started at 0:00, showing the same segment
  every time. Now each video clip starts at a random position,
  making the frame feel more dynamic. The random start is at
  least (interval) seconds before the video end, ensuring the
  full display duration plays through without clipping.
